**Where are the most promising grid connection opportunities for your next BESS project?**

As competition for grid connection capacity intensifies across Germany, selecting the right connection point is becoming one of the most critical decisions in BESS development.

Yet identifying promising substations requires more than assessing available capacity. Grid congestion, planned network reinforcements, connection timelines and curtailment exposure can all influence a project's development prospects and investment risk.

To bring these factors together, Revolt Energy is launching **GridScore™ — a grid intelligence platform designed to help BESS developers and investors screen, compare and prioritize grid connection opportunities across Germany.**

## From Fragmented Grid Data to Actionable Intelligence

Grid connection assessments often require developers to navigate multiple sources of network information, understand evolving regulatory frameworks and evaluate technical constraints across different locations.

GridScore brings relevant indicators together in a single workspace, providing a more structured approach to grid connection screening and project prioritization.

Instead of evaluating substations in isolation, developers can compare opportunities across a range of grid-related factors and focus development resources where they are most likely to create value.

## What Can You Do with GridScore?

**Identify and compare connection opportunities**

Explore substations across Germany, assess available grid capacity indicators and compare potential connection locations.

**Understand grid congestion and curtailment exposure**

Review historical congestion and redispatch patterns to identify network constraints that may affect project performance.

**Track grid expansion plans and timelines**

Explore planned network reinforcements, including relevant measures from Germany's Network Development Plan (NEP), and assess how future grid developments may influence connection opportunities.

**Prioritize your development pipeline**

Compare substations using GridScore's structured scoring framework to support early-stage screening and investment decisions.

**Stay informed about grid connection developments**

Monitor relevant grid developments and application windows to help inform project planning and connection strategies.

## From 380 kV to 110 kV: Expanding Coverage

GridScore is launching with intelligence covering Germany's 380 kV transmission network, with ongoing expansion into the 110 kV distribution grid.

The platform is being extended progressively to selected distribution system operators, supporting developers as grid connection procedures and capacity allocation frameworks evolve.

The availability and precision of individual indicators depend on the information published by the relevant grid operators.

## Built for BESS Developers and Investors

GridScore is designed to support teams managing multiple development opportunities, screening project pipelines or evaluating grid-related investment risks.

By combining infrastructure mapping, grid capacity indicators, network development plans and congestion analysis, the platform helps turn fragmented grid information into more informed development decisions.

GridScore supports early-stage screening and prioritization. It does not replace formal grid connection studies, binding capacity confirmations or project-specific technical due diligence.

For projects requiring deeper assessment, Revolt also provides independent grid risk assessments and specialist grid advisory services.
